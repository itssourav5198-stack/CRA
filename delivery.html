Delivery Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short: Campus Battle Arena is a digital service. There is nothing to ship; everything is delivered online by email and on the website.

1. No physical shipping

We do not sell or ship physical goods. The service you pay for is participation in an online competition.

2. What you receive and when

ItemHow and when
Registration confirmationBy email to your registered address once your payment succeeds
Event access and instructionsOn the website and by email before the event starts
Results and leaderboardOn the website after scoring and verification
E-certificateBy email after results are declared, for every valid submission
Prize money (winners)UPI or bank transfer within 15 days of result verification

3. If you do not receive something

Check your spam or promotions folder first, and make sure you entered your email correctly at registration. If it is still missing, write to lyroweb.solution@gmail.com with your name, registered email and payment ID, and we will resend or correct it.
