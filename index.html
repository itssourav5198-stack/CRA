HELP CENTRE

Everything you need to know

Can’t find your answer? Email us at the address in the footer, or read our Terms, Privacy and Refund policies.

Who can join?

Any college or school student in India. You can join solo or form a squad of up to 4, even across colleges.

What if you cancel the registration?

You can't get any refund if you cancel the registration by your own.

What if the event is cancelled?

You get a full refund within 7–10 working days. Failed or duplicate payments are refunded too.

Can I use AI tools for the design battle?

Yes. Note which tools you used in your submission description. Copying another site wholesale is not allowed.

How are prizes paid?

By UPI or bank transfer within 15 days of result verification. We may ask for your college ID or national ID for age verification.
