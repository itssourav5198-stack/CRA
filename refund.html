Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short: if we cancel the event or charged twice, you get your money back. If you cancel your registration, the fee is not refundable.

1. Cancellation by you

If you cancel your registration or choose not to take part, the fee is not refundable. Please check the dates, format and rules before you pay.

2. Cancellation of the event by us

If we cancel the event (or a track you paid for), you receive a full refund of that fee within 7–10 working days. You do not need to ask; we will email you. If we only reschedule, your registration stays valid for the new date.

3. Failed or duplicate payments

  • Payment failed but money was debited: the amount is normally reversed automatically by your bank or Cashfree within a few working days. If it is not, write to us with your payment ID or screenshot and we will follow it up with Cashfree.
  • Paid twice for the same registration: the extra payment is refunded in full within 7–10 working days of verification.

4. Disqualification

If you are disqualified for cheating, copied work or breaking the rules, the fee is not refunded.

5. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet). Your bank may take a few additional days to show the credit after we process it.

6. How to ask for a refund or report a problem

Email lyroweb.solution@gmail.com with the subject "Refund request", along with your name, registered email, phone number and payment ID or screenshot. Please write within 7 days of the payment so we can verify it quickly.
