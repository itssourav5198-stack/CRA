LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to run the event, we never sell, rent or trade your personal data, and payments are handled by Cashfree, so we never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login details.

1. Who is responsible for your data

LyroWeb Solutions, which organises Campus Battle Arena, decides why and how your data is used (the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). Contact: lyroweb.solution@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

DataWhy we need it
Name, email, phone, collegeTo register you, send confirmations, run the event and issue certificates
Track, solo or squad, squad name or invite codeTo place you in the right track and team
Quiz answers, timing and activity signals such as tab switchesTo score the quiz and detect cheating
Design submission (live link, GitHub repo, description, AI tools used)To judge and rank entries
Payment status, payment ID and amountTo confirm your registration and handle refunds. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Cashfree's page and are not stored by us
UPI or bank details, and college or government ID (winners only, when requested)To verify age and student status and to pay prizes
Basic technical data (IP address, browser, device)To keep the site secure and working
Emails you send usTo reply and resolve issues

3. How we use it

  • Run the event: registration, scoring, leaderboards, judging and results.
  • Send service messages: payment confirmation, event reminders, results, certificates.
  • Prevent cheating, fraud and misuse.
  • Pay prizes, handle refunds and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build marketing profiles. We send promotional messages about future events only if you agree to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

4. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone, ever. We share data only in the limited cases below.

5. Who we share data with

  • Payment partner: Cashfree Payments processes your payment under its own privacy policy and security standards.
  • Service providers that help us run the site and send emails (for example hosting and email delivery). They may use your data only to provide that service to us.
  • Judges and organising team: only what is needed to evaluate and run the event.
  • Public leaderboard and announcements: your name, college and score or rank may be shown. Tell us at lyroweb.solution@gmail.com if you want a different display name.
  • Authorities: when required by law or a valid legal order, or to protect rights and safety.

Your phone number, email address and ID documents are never published.

6. Cookies, browser storage and third-party fonts

Our site does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to keep your registration draft and display preference on your own device. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google may receive your IP address when a page loads.

7. How long we keep data

  • Registration, quiz and submission data: only as long as needed for the event, results, disputes and certificates, and up to 24 months after the event unless you ask us to delete it earlier.
  • Payment, refund and prize records: for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • ID documents shared for verification: deleted once verification and prize payout are complete, unless the law requires us to keep them.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, access controls and trusted providers to protect your data. No system is 100% secure, but if a breach affecting your data occurs we will act promptly and inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights

You can write to lyroweb.solution@gmail.com to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • ask us to delete it (we may keep what the law requires us to keep);
  • withdraw your consent for optional uses such as promotional emails;
  • raise a grievance. We aim to reply within 7 days and resolve it within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

10. Age

CBA is for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has registered, tell us and we will delete the data.

11. Transfers

Our providers may store or process data on servers outside India. We use providers with appropriate safeguards and do not transfer data to any country restricted by the Government of India.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you by email or on the site.
